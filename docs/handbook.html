Anno 1602 Extensions

Bigger worlds, stronger rivals, cheaper building, more life at sea, six campaigns with a story – one of them in a giant world –, 15 challenges, random and daily adventures, scenarios of your own, events, time limits and a logbook next to the game. Everything runs in memory, no game file is changed.

Setting sail

  1. Open “Anno 1602 – Extensions” on the desktop (on a German Windows: “Anno 1602 – Erweiterungen”).
  2. On the New game tab choose the options or use a preset: Everything big (maximum), For campaigns (campaigns as intended), Giant world (the World Empire on 2000 × 1400), Relaxed (build in peace), Tough test (rivals and pirates at their strongest) or Reset to defaults.
  3. Play with extensions. In the game Single Player → New Game: the endless game at the top, the campaigns at the very bottom under Own Scenarios.
  4. Press F7 for up to 16× speed, F8 for ultra speed (up to 128×, option “Ultra”). After attack messages the game switches back by itself; then press the key again.
  5. To continue later: tab Continue, select the save, Play with these settings, then Load Game in the game. The save is loaded with exactly the options it was made with.

Launcher option “Events” · new

Events

With Rare something happens about every 3 game hours, with Often about every game hour – but only once you have a town of your own, and only while the game clock runs: a pause adds nothing, turbo speeds it up. The tool window triggers the events, so it must stay open. Every event appears in the logbook with its effect.

A patron+2,000 to +10,000 gold – the higher your residents have risen, the more.
Fire in the warehouseThe repairs cost 5 % of your gold (only with at least 2,000 gold).
A trader’s gift10 to 30 t tools, wood, bricks or food in your biggest town, as much as its storage holds.
Tax windfall · Tax slumpFor one game hour taxes bring in half as much again, or a quarter less.
Bumper harvest · Marsh feverFor one game hour all farms and workshops work half as fast again, or a quarter slower.

Taxes and production are the same for everybody in the game: the rivals gain or lose with you. Gold and goods are real changes and are saved with the game. A tax or production event ends after one game hour, when you load or start another game, when the game ends or when the tool window closes – the values are then exactly as before.

Launcher tab “Adventures” · new

Unlocking chapters one by one

Below the list in the Adventures tab is the check box Unlock chapters one by one (off at first). When it is ticked, the game’s list holds of each campaign only the chapters up to the first one you have not won yet. The later ones are shown in the launcher in grey with a lock (“🔒 locked – win chapter 2 first”). After a win the launcher adds the next chapter when it starts again (“just unlocked”). Untick the box and every chapter is back in the game. Challenges and adventures are never locked.

The wins are recorded by the logbook – so keep it open while you play a chapter (or tick “Open the logbook with the game”).

Against the Clock · The Royal Deadline · your own scenarios · new

Time limits

Some missions must be fulfilled in time: the challenges “Against the Clock” (3 hours) and “The Royal Deadline” (6 hours), and every scenario of your own with a time limit. When the time runs out, the mission is lost.

Only with the extensionsThe game itself cannot load a time limit from a scenario. The tool sets it at every start and at every load of a savegame. With Start original the same scenario has no limit.
The game’s own clockWhile a limit is set, the game shows its own clock at the bottom left: the elapsed game time in minutes and seconds. “180:00” is the end of a 3-hour limit, “360:00” of a 6-hour one.
Time left in the logbookFor example “Time limit: 2 h 44 min left (clock 15:03 of 180:00)”.
Game time, not real timeA pause stops the clock, turbo makes it run faster – it wins you no time. At normal speed one hour on the clock takes about 66 minutes.

Random adventures

A fresh island world with a fresh mission whenever you like – made by the same builder as the campaigns, with a short story and the helmsman’s advice.

  1. In the launcher open the tab Adventures.
  2. Choose the difficulty: Easy, Average, Demanding or Hard. Optionally enter a number from 0 to 999,999; without one, a number is rolled for you.
  3. Roll an adventure. The box shows the name, for example “Adventure 4711 Demanding”, and your mission. In the game it waits under Own Scenarios.

Extra goalsNone on Easy, up to two on Hard: balance, trade balance, a good in store, total residents, another town

Small and big maps

Every scenario – also the random and daily adventures and your own – has two versions. With Start original the classic map with mostly 17 to 20 islands applies. With Play with extensions, map size 1000 × 700 or bigger and More islands the big version applies: up to 50 islands, more resources and natives. The World Empire, Seven Seas, Around the World and your own scenarios with the giant world box carry a third version: from map size 2000 × 1400 on (preset Giant world) the 50 islands lie in five island realms. The helmsman’s advice

Build a warehouse earlyWith strong pirates the game is over as soon as your only ship sinks and you have no settlement.
Gold options and campaignsYour starting gold and the rivals’ gold replace the scenario’s gold. The “For campaigns” button sets both to “As per difficulty”.
Saves from the big worldLoad them only with the extensions and the same map size – easiest through the Continue tab. Leave the black window open until the game has ended: only then are the save’s options noted.
Events need the black windowIt triggers them and puts taxes and production back afterwards. Closing it ends a running event; the game goes on.
Relaxed and Tough testBoth presets are made for the endless game. In a scenario their gold settings replace the scenario’s gold, and “Every crop on every island” does not apply there. For campaigns use “For campaigns”.
