Anno 1602 Extensions

Bigger worlds, stronger rivals, cheaper building, more life at sea, three campaigns with a story, random adventures and a logbook next to the game. Everything runs in memory, no game file is changed.

Setting sail

  1. Open “Anno 1602 – Extensions” on the desktop (on a German Windows: “Anno 1602 – Erweiterungen”).
  2. On the New game tab choose the options or use a preset: Everything big (maximum), For campaigns (campaigns as intended), Relaxed (build in peace), Tough test (rivals and pirates at their strongest) or Reset to defaults.
  3. Play with extensions. In the game Single Player → New Game: the endless game at the top, the campaigns at the very bottom under Own Scenarios.
  4. Press F7 for up to 16× speed. After attack messages the game switches back by itself; then press F7 again.
  5. To continue later: tab Continue, select the save, Play with these settings, then Load Game in the game. The save is loaded with exactly the options it was made with.

The Storm Sea

A storm wrecks your convoy on the reefs of the Storm Sea. With one battered ship you build a colony, win the trade of the islands, fight off pirates and a treacherous trading house, and in the end unite the islands under one crown. Every chapter asks for something different. New in November 2026: the difficulty is designed by analogy with the measured chapters and has not been played through yet.

1

Only the “Morning Star” survived the storm – with a half-empty hold and hardly any gold. The castaways of the other ships wait on a foreign shore.

A town of 200 residents, 100 of them settlers. A second settlement on another island for the castaways. Positive balance.

TipOnly 10,000 gold, 20 t tools, 12 t wood and 8 t food. Build foresters first – they cost no wood. If tools run short, set them to “buy” in the warehouse; the free traders bring them.

Launcher tab “Adventures” · new

Random adventures

A fresh island world with a fresh mission whenever you like – made by the same builder as the campaigns, with a short story and the helmsman’s advice.

  1. In the launcher open the tab Adventures.
  2. Choose the difficulty: Easy, Average, Demanding or Hard. Optionally enter a number from 0 to 999,999; without one, a number is rolled for you.
  3. Roll an adventure. The box shows the name, for example “Adventure 4711 Demanding”, and your mission. In the game it waits under Own Scenarios.
RolledPossibilities
MapClassic (some islands larger), an archipelago of 26 to 30 small islands (the largest 50 × 52, so that the rivals can settle too), or a few huge continents
Main goalTowns, a realm of many settlements, trade (goods and trade balance), a monopoly, or conquest (not on Easy)
Extra goalsNone on Easy, up to two on Hard: balance, trade balance, a good in store, total residents, another town
WorldOre, gold and crops to match the mission, native villages, your and the rivals’ starting gold, the rivals’ strength
PiratesNever on Easy, always on Hard, sometimes in between
ShipsRich start cargo on Easy, sometimes lean cargo on Hard; extra ships where the mission needs them

The same number with the same difficulty always gives the same adventure, in English and in German – play a map again or give the number to a friend. The same number with another difficulty replaces the file.

Launcher button “Open logbook” · new

The logbook

A small window next to the game. It only reads the game’s memory and never changes anything; it may run before, during and after the game and connects by itself. Play in window mode or put it on a second monitor.

MissionEvery goal with a live progress bar, green when met – the same check the game uses. In a race also how close the rivals are.
Your empireGold, balance and trade balance, towns, residents per class, workers and ships.
RivalsBest town, residents, towns, ships and gold of each rival.
Achievements24 milestones, kept with date and game in logbook.json. Won missions also show up in the launcher’s Adventures tab.

Small and big maps

Every scenario and every random adventure has two versions. With Start original the classic map with mostly 17 to 20 islands applies. With Play with extensions, map size 1000 × 700 or bigger and More islands the big version applies: up to 50 islands, more resources and natives. A tick above only stores in this browser which chapters you have completed.

The helmsman’s advice

Build a warehouse earlyWith strong pirates the game is over as soon as your only ship sinks and you have no settlement.
Gold options and campaignsYour starting gold and the rivals’ gold replace the scenario’s gold. The “For campaigns” button sets both to “As per difficulty”.
Saves from the big worldLoad them only with the extensions and the same map size – easiest through the Continue tab. Leave the black window open until the game has ended: only then are the save’s options noted.
Relaxed and Tough testBoth presets are made for the endless game. In a scenario their gold settings replace the scenario’s gold, and “Every crop on every island” does not apply there. For campaigns use “For campaigns”.
To another PCUnzip the ZIP from GitHub (Code → Download ZIP), double-click install.cmd.
